Coach registration

Every coach — including a parent who wants to help coach — must register here to get coach dashboard access. Registration is reviewed and approved by your club before access is granted.

What kind of coach are you?

Every coach can see their athletes' names, divisions and status, enter athletes into meets, and see results. Private registration details stay with the team admin.

Background check

Background checks are valid for 2 years and must fall within the last 2 years of the current season. If you can't provide a verifiable date, you'll have 90 days to complete one before losing coach access.

VYC rule IX.2.f.7: every coach completes SafeSport. If you completed it with another organization (for example USATF), that certificate counts — enter its date.

By registering as a coach I agree to follow the Valley Youth Conference Code of Conduct and zero-tolerance policy for fighting, drugs, and alcohol, to complete a background check as required, and to submit results and meet entries only for my own team.

Team Admin registration

Team admins run their team in this app: they do the first review of athlete registrations (including documents and medical information) before their Conference President certifies them, approve the team's coaches, approve transfer requests, and manage meets. Your request is reviewed and approved by a VYC site admin or your team's existing team admin before access is granted.

Do you hold any of these positions?

VYC board positions (elected every two years, confirmed in the app each season by a site admin). The two Conference Presidents certify the rosters — they are the only people who can certify or change an athlete, each for their own conference. The Commissioner approves transfers, late additions and fines. The Treasurer receives fees and records payments.

Background check

Team admins can see athletes' private information, so the same background-check rules as coaches apply: valid for 2 years (3 years for USATF-certified coaches). If you can't provide a verifiable date, you'll have 90 days to complete one.

By registering as a team admin I agree to follow the Valley Youth Conference Code of Conduct and zero-tolerance policy for fighting, drugs, and alcohol, to complete a background check as required, to keep athletes' and families' private information confidential and use it only for team business, and to act only for my own team.

Timer registration

Timers download each meet's entries (the Hy-Tek file for Meet Manager) and upload the meet's results. That's all a timer account can do — no team rosters, athlete records or entering athletes into meets. Timers work across teams, so there's no team to pick — your registration is reviewed and approved by a VYC site admin.

By registering as a timer I agree to follow the Valley Youth Conference Code of Conduct, to upload only official, accurate meet results, and to not share or alter results.

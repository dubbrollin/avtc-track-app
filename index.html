Code of Conduct

Zero-Tolerance Policy. The Valley Youth Conference has a zero-tolerance policy for fighting, drugs, and alcohol. Any athlete, parent, or coach found fighting, in possession of or under the influence of drugs or alcohol, or providing drugs or alcohol to a minor, at any practice, meet, or team-related event, will be immediately removed from the program with no refund of fees. This policy exists to protect every child in this program and the volunteers who run it. Additional conduct policies (spectator conduct, coach conduct, and appeals) are published on the Conference website, valleyconference.org, and are incorporated into this agreement by reference.

Background check

Background checks are valid for 2 years and must fall within the last 2 years of the current season. If you can't provide a verifiable date, you'll have 90 days to complete one before losing coach access.

By registering as a coach I agree to follow the Valley Youth Conference Code of Conduct and zero-tolerance policy for fighting, drugs, and alcohol, to complete a background check as required, and to submit results and meet entries only for my own team.

Valley United Striders — Postseason Team

Valley United Striders is VYC's postseason all-star team (valleyunitedstriders.com). Only VYC IEC qualified athletes will be invited on the team!! All High School athletes are welcome. This is a separate application and a separate agreement from the regular-season Valley Conference contract.

Athlete Information

Eligibility

Eligibility: an athlete must have already run this season for a Valley Conference team to run with Valley United Striders, unless they are age 15 or above.

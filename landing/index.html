What it does

Everything a track club does, in one app

No more paper packets, spreadsheets of who paid, group texts about who's running, or waiting until Monday for results. Each person sees exactly what their role needs.

Register & certify

Parents register online with documents, e-signatures and medical details. The team reviews, the league certifies, and returning athletes fill in from last season.

Collect team fees

Build your fee list with discounts. Families pay by card or payment plan, or mark Zelle, Cash App, Venmo, check or cash as sent. You see who's paid.

Run every meet

Parents say who's coming, coaches enter athletes by event, and entries go straight into Hy-Tek Meet Manager. Results come back live during the meet.

Keep families in the loop

Team messages with photos and polls, phone alerts, a team page with news and a store, volunteer sign-ups and a livestream link for meet day.

A season, start to finish

From sign-up day to league finals

The app follows the way a youth track season actually runs.

  1. Register

    Families sign up and upload proof of birth. Teams can import athletes from Team Manager, CSV or Excel.

  2. Review & certify

    The team admin checks each athlete, then the league certifies. Transfers get signed off by both teams.

  3. Pay

    Families see their total line by line and pay the way your team accepts.

  4. Compete

    RSVPs, entries, volunteer jobs and labels for every meet. Results post live from the timing table.

  5. Rank & qualify

    Season bests, rankings, records and conference standings update after every meet, ready for the postseason.

Main features

Built from a real season, not a sales pitch

Every feature here is already in use by clubs running real meets.

Online registration

Proof of birth, insurance and physical details, emergency contacts, and signed agreements, code of conduct and consent forms.

Returning athletes

Type a last name and reuse last season's details. Returning athletes on the same team skip proof of birth.

Import from other systems

Bring in a Team Manager roster, CSV or Excel file. Families get a code to connect and finish in a couple of minutes.

Fees & payments

Card checkout and payment plans through Stripe, paid straight to the team, plus Zelle, Cash App, Venmo, check and cash tracking.

Schedule & RSVPs

Every meet by week, in a list or calendar, with directions. Families answer Coming or Missing before a deadline you set.

Meet entries

Entry by event with relays, alternates, event limits per division and entry deadlines. Hy-Tek entry file in one tap.

Live results

Results stream in from Meet Manager during the meet. The Live tab refreshes about every 30 seconds.

Rankings & records

Season bests, rankings by division, top 50 per event, all-time records and conference standings, updated after every meet.

Meet labels

Award, personal-best, record-breaker, participation, competitor and mailing labels on standard Avery sheets.

Team messenger

Group and direct chats with photos, videos, files and polls. Alerts by phone notification, email or text.

Team pages

Each club gets its own page: about, practice info, coaches, news, a photo gallery and a team store.

Volunteer sign-ups

Host teams post meet-day jobs by division or time shift. Families pick a spot, and open jobs are filled automatically.

Livestream

Paste an unlisted YouTube Live link and families watch from the app. The replay stays afterwards.

Private by role

Team admins see full records. Other coaches see names, divisions and status; division coaches see only their division.

Cross country too

Run a cross country season alongside track, including a postseason team with its own sign-up.

Works with Hy-Tek

Keep the timing software you already use

The app talks to Hy-Tek Meet Manager and Team Manager, so your timers don't learn anything new and results are never typed twice.

  1. Entries out. Download the meet's entries and import them into Meet Manager.
  2. Results in, live. The timing laptop sends results as each event finishes.
  3. Backup up. Upload the Meet Manager backup after the meet; the newest one always wins.
  4. Rosters in. Bring athletes over from a Team Manager roster export.

Questions

Good to know

Can my club use it now?

A version for other clubs is being prepared now. Tell us about your club with the form below and we'll be in touch as soon as it's ready, or sooner to talk about your season.

Will it have our club's name and colours?

Yes. The version for other clubs starts blank, so your families see your club's name, colours and logo, not someone else's.

What does it cost?

Pricing for other clubs hasn't been set yet. Send us a few details about your club and we'll get back to you with what fits your size.

Do families have to download anything?

No. It works in any phone or computer browser. Families can also add it to their home screen so it opens like an app. App Store and Google Play versions are on the way.

Does it work with Hy-Tek Meet Manager and Team Manager?

Yes. Meet entries download in Hy-Tek format, results come back from Meet Manager live or from a file, meet backups upload in one step, and Team Manager rosters can be imported.

How do card payments work?

Each team connects its own Stripe account, so card payments and payment plans go straight to the team's bank. Stripe charges its standard card fee. Teams can also take Zelle, Cash App, Venmo, check or cash and mark them received.

Can it run a whole league, not just one club?

Yes. That's how it's used today: league officials certify athletes, approve transfers, set up the schedule and event limits, track league fees, and keep records and conference standings across every club.

Who can see a child's private details?

Access depends on the role. Team admins can see full registrations, including documents. Other coaches see names, divisions and status only, and division coaches see just their own division. Parents see only their own children.

Can we move our athletes over from another system?

Yes. Upload a Team Manager roster or a CSV or Excel file. Each family gets a 6-letter code, connects with it, and finishes a short form to complete their registration.

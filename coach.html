Roster

You do the first review: check the proof of birth and details, then mark the athlete Team reviewed. Your Conference President gives the final Certified — only certified athletes can be entered in meets. Only the president can change an athlete's information.

You're seeing names, divisions and status only — full registration details (documents, contact info, medical info) are visible to team admins only.

Coaches on my team

Approve a new coach registration, make a coach a team admin, or review background-check status. Only team admins can do this.

Transfer requests

Athletes from your team last season who are registering with another team. Confirm they're in good standing (no balance owed, no Code of Conduct issue — rule IV.A.3.c) or report the issue. The joining team and the Commissioner must also approve before the athlete can be certified.

Account & sign-in

How do I sign in? Is there a magic link?

No magic link — sign in with your email and password, or tap Continue with Google. First time? Choose First time? Create password.

It says "Wrong email/password." What do I do?

Usually it means you haven't set a password yet. Tap First time? Create password with the same email you registered with. If you signed up with Google, use Continue with Google instead.

I never got the confirmation email.

Check your spam or promotions folder, then tap Send it again on the sign-in screen. The link must be opened before you can sign in.

Which email should I use?

The same email you put on your athlete's registration. That's how your dashboard finds your athletes — and how imported athletes link to you automatically.

Can I look around before making an account?

Yes. On the sign-in box, use Look around without an account to see the app as a coach, team admin, parent or timer. It's view-only.

Registration

What do I need before I start registering?
  • Proof of birth (birth certificate, state ID, passport or similar) as a photo or PDF — new athletes and transfers only
  • Insurance carrier
  • Date of the athlete's physical and the doctor's name
  • An emergency contact

You'll sign the athlete agreement, code of conduct and parent consent on screen. Step-by-step guide

Which division is my athlete in?

It's based on age on December 31 of the season year (season year minus birth year). The form fills it in for you.

DivisionAges
Sub-Gremlin5–6
Gremlin7–8
Bantam9–10
Juniors11–12
Youth13–14
Intermediates15–18

Athletes compete only in their own division — no moving up or down.

Does my returning athlete need proof of birth again?

Not if they're returning to the same team. Enter the same name and birth date as last season and you'll see ✓ Returning athlete… No proof of birth needed. Athletes switching teams count as new and must upload it.

How recent does the physical need to be?

It must be dated on or after May 30 of the year before the season.

Can a high school athlete run VYC?

No. Athletes running on a high school track team (CIF or City) can't register for VYC. The form asks this for athletes 13 and older.

What do the statuses on my dashboard mean?
  • Awaiting team review — your team admin hasn't checked it yet.
  • Team reviewed — awaiting certification — approved by the team; the Conference President checks it next.
  • Certified — all set. Only certified athletes can be entered in meets.
  • Rejected — something needs fixing; contact your team admin.
My team says they already registered my athlete. Do I have to fill out everything again?

No. Sign in to your Parent Dashboard. If the team used your email, your athlete is already waiting; otherwise enter the 6-letter code from your team plus your athlete's birthday. Tap Finish registration → — the form is pre-filled, so you just check it, fill any blanks and sign. Guide

My code doesn't work.

Check that the birthday matches exactly what your team has on file, and that you typed the 6-letter code correctly (dashes and capital letters don't matter). If it still fails, ask your team admin to check or add your email.

What happens if we register late?

Registrations after the league's certification date are marked late, and the team is charged a late fee. Once registration closes for the season, an athlete can only be added with a late pass approved by the league. Athletes your team imported before the certification date count as on time, even if you finish the form later.

My athlete is switching teams. What's different?

Register on the new team as a new athlete and upload proof of birth. Before certification, the former team confirms good standing, the new team accepts the athlete, and the Commissioner approves. Valley United Striders athletes return to last season's team unless that team approves the move.

I have more than one child.

After submitting, tap Yes — register another child. Your family details carry over. Some teams also give a sibling discount on the fee.

Fees & payments

How much is the fee?

Each team sets its own fee. You'll see it under your athlete on the Parent Dashboard. If it isn't posted yet, your team will let you know.

How can I pay?

Depending on your team: card (secure Stripe checkout), a payment plan with automatic payments, or Zelle, Cash App, Venmo, check or cash. For those, send the money and tap I sent it so the team can confirm it. Guide

My payment-plan card failed.

Your fee box will show Update my card. Tap it to add a new card; the plan carries on.

I paid but it still says I owe.

Card payments update right away. For Zelle, Cash App, Venmo, check or cash, it shows Payment sent — awaiting team confirmation until your team marks it received. Contact your team admin if it's been a while.

Meets & results

How do I tell the coaches whether we're coming?

On your dashboard under Every meet this season, tap Coming or Missing. The deadline is Thursday 8 PM before the meet. Later answers are late adds and run at the end of each heat.

Can I choose my athlete's events?

If your team allows it, you can pick 1–3 preferred events under details. Coaches make the final entries.

How many events can an athlete enter?

Sub-Gremlin and Gremlin: 3 per meet. Bantam, Juniors and Youth: 4, and the 4th must include a relay. Intermediates: 4.

What time do meets start?

Field events start at 8:30 AM and the first race (3000m) at 8:45 AM. See every date and location on the Schedule.

Where do I see results?

On Results: Live (meet day, refreshes about every 30 seconds), Meet Results, Season Rankings and All-Time Records. Your own athlete's bests are on your dashboard under Season bests.

My athlete ran in a mixed race. Why is their place different?

Mixed races are filed under each runner's own division and gender, so the place shown is among their own group. You'll also see "ran in the mixed race, Nth overall".

Why do results say "🔒 Results locked"?

The league can lock a team's results while fees are unpaid. Contact your team admin.

Do I have to volunteer?

Every VYC family is asked to volunteer at each meet. Pick a job from the Volunteers tab on your dashboard. Jobs still open by Thursday 8 PM are assigned automatically.

App & alerts

Is there an app to download?

Yes — install it straight from the site. Android/Chrome: tap Install app on Home. iPhone: open the site in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

How do I get message notifications on my phone?

Open team messages, tap 🔔 My alerts, turn on Notify this device and allow notifications. You can also choose email or text alerts. Guide

I'm on iPhone and I'm not getting notifications.

iPhone only sends notifications to the Home Screen app. Add the site to your Home Screen from Safari, open it from that icon, then turn on Notify this device again.

I don't see Team messages, Livestream, Photos or Store.

Those are team extras your team turns on. If your team doesn't have them, they won't appear.

Coaches & team admins

What do I need to coach?

A current background check (good for 2 years, or 3 for USATF) and SafeSport. Head Coaches must be 21 or older. Sign up on Register → Coach. Guide

Who approves my coach account?

Your team admin or a site admin approves coaches. Team admins are approved by a site admin or the team's existing team admin.

Why can't I see all of my athletes' details?

Team admins see everything. Other coaches see names, divisions and status; Division Coaches see only their own division.

Who certifies athletes?

The team admin marks each athlete ✓ Team reviewed, then a Conference President certifies. The Commissioner approves transfers and late additions.

Why are my meet entries locked?

Entries stay locked until the league Treasurer confirms your team's certification fees. Only certified athletes can be entered.

We registered our athletes in another system. Can we move them over?

Yes. Use Import athletes under Team admin with a Team Manager roster (.zip or .tcl) or a CSV/Excel export. Parents get a code to finish a short form. Guide

What are the paid extras?

$99 per team per season: Team Messenger, online payments and tracking, live results, livestream, photo gallery and team store. The team page and volunteer duties are always free.

Can we get the Meet Manager backup from a meet we visited?

Yes. Coaches and team admins of every team at the meet can download it from Meet Manager backups or Backup ⬇ in Our schedule.

Timers

Do I still need to upload the backup if results went up live?

Yes — always. After the meet, File › Backup in Meet Manager and upload the Tfmm6Bkup….zip. Guide

My upload says "That's a Team Manager export."

You picked a Team Manager results or entries file. Upload the Meet Manager backup instead (File › Backup in Meet Manager).

We fixed results after uploading. What now?

Upload the newer backup. It replaces that meet's results completely, so the latest backup is always what's posted.

Can I type in a result by hand?

No. Results only come from Meet Manager — the live connection, a results file, or the backup.

Something was posted by mistake.

Ask your team admin to remove it. Only a site admin can remove a backup.

Didn't find it? Try the How-To guides, ask your team admin, or see league rules at valleyconference.org.

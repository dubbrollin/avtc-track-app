How-To Guides

Step-by-step help for parents, coaches, team admins and meet timers. Pick who you are, then open a guide. Quick answers are on the FAQ.

Everything a parent does happens on two pages: Register (once a season per athlete) and your Parent Dashboard (all season long).

🔑Create your account and sign inEmail + password, or Google
  1. Go to Home and find Already registered? Sign in.
  2. First time here? Enter your email, choose First time? Create password and pick a password (6+ characters). Or tap Continue with Google.
  3. Open the confirmation email we send and click the link. Not there? Check spam, or tap Send it again.
  4. Sign in. Parents land on the Parent Dashboard; coaches and team admins land on the Coach Dashboard.

New parents: Register asks just two questions (you're a parent, and your athlete's team), then you create your sign-in and land on your Parent Dashboard.

✏️Register your athleteAbout 10 minutes the first time
  1. First time? Open Register, choose Parent / Guardian, pick your athlete's team, and create your sign-in.
  2. On your Parent Dashboard, tap + Register an athlete. Your team is already filled in — pick the athlete's gender. Enter the athlete's name and date of birth — the division fills in by itself.
    • Returning athlete? Start typing the last name and tap Yes, this is my athlete to reuse last season's details.
  3. Fill in address, phone, email and an emergency contact, and answer the returning / last-season-team questions.
  4. Proof of birth: choose the document type and upload a photo or PDF. Returning athletes on the same team usually skip this — you'll see ✓ Returning athlete… No proof of birth needed.
  5. Insurance & Medical: insurance carrier, date of the physical exam and the doctor's name.
  6. Parent / Guardian: Parent 1 is required, Parent 2 is optional.
  7. Read and check the Athlete's Agreement, Code of Conduct and Parent/Guardian Consent. Sign with Draw or Type name.
  8. Tap Submit registration. Then choose Yes — register another child (keeps your family details) or go to the payment portal.

Deadline: the registration must be completed — every document uploaded and both signatures — by the certification date shown at the top of the form. Starting it earlier doesn't count; anything completed after that date is late.

Athletes 13 and older who run for a high school track team can't register for VYC.

🔗Your team already sent your athlete? Finish in 2 minutesFor athletes the team moved over from another system
  1. Sign in to the Parent Dashboard.
  2. If the team used your email, you'll see Your team already sent… right away. Skip to step 4.
  3. Otherwise open Got a code from your team?, type the 6-letter code from your team's message and your athlete's date of birth, and tap Connect.
  4. Tap Finish registration →. The form opens already filled in — check it, fill any blanks (emergency contact, insurance, physical), and sign.

Finish before the certification date. A registration only counts as on time once it's completed — every document uploaded and both signatures — by that date. Your team sending it early doesn't count.

💳Pay your team feeCard, payment plan, Zelle, Cash App, Venmo, check or cash
  1. On the Parent Dashboard, look at the fee box under your athlete (or open Pay Team Fee).
  2. Choose how to pay — the choices depend on your team:
    • Pay $… by card — secure Stripe checkout, money goes straight to the team.
    • Payment plan — a first payment today, then automatic payments on the schedule shown.
    • Other ways to pay — send by Zelle, Cash App, Venmo or check/cash, then tap I sent it.
  3. The box shows Paid in full ✓ when the team has it, then disappears.

If your team hasn't turned on online payments, the box shows the amount and says your team will tell you how and when to pay.

✅Track your athlete's approvalTeam review, then league certification
  1. Awaiting team review — your team admin hasn't looked yet.
  2. Team reviewed — awaiting certification — the team approved it; now the Conference President checks it.
  3. Certified — done. Only certified athletes can be entered in meets.

If you see Rejected, contact your team admin — it's usually a missing or unreadable document.

📅Tell the coaches who's coming to a meetDeadline: Thursday 8 PM before the meet
  1. On the Parent Dashboard, find Every meet this season.
  2. Tap Coming or Missing for each athlete and meet. Use details to add a note or, if your team allows it, pick the events you'd like.
  3. Change your mind? Use Update answer any time before the deadline.

Coaches make the final event entries. Answers after Thursday 8 PM are late adds and run at the end of each heat.

🙋Sign up to volunteer at a meetEvery family is asked to help at each meet
  1. On your dashboard, open the Volunteers tab or tap Pick a spot → on the reminder.
  2. Choose an open job and save. Jobs still open by Thursday 8 PM are assigned automatically.
🏅See results and season bests
  1. Your dashboard's Season bests shows each event's best mark — NEW BEST and Q (qualifying) badges included — and updates after every meet.
  2. For full meet results, rankings and records, open Results. On meet day the 🔴 Live tab refreshes about every 30 seconds.
🔔Install the app and get message alerts on your phone
  1. Install the app. Android / Chrome: on Home tap Install app. iPhone: open the site in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then open it from the new icon.
  2. In your team messages, tap 🔔 My alerts.
  3. Turn on Notify this device and allow notifications when your phone asks. You can also choose Email me or Text me.

On iPhone, alerts only work from the Home Screen app — not from a Safari tab. Team messages are available when your team has the paid extras on.

Coaches work from the Coach Dashboard. Team admins also get Team admin: roster & intake for the roster, coaches, payments and imports.

🧢Sign up as a coach or team adminBackground check + SafeSport required
  1. Open Register and choose Coach or Team Admin, pick your team and tap Continue.
  2. Choose your team and coach type (Head Coach must be 21+), and enter your background check and SafeSport dates. Need a check? Use Start your VYC background check →.
  3. Submit and wait for approval. Your team admin (or a site admin) approves coaches; a site admin approves team admins.
🗂️Review your team's registrationsTeam admins
  1. On the Coach Dashboard tap Team admin: roster & intake → Roster & intake.
  2. Filter to Needs team review. For each athlete, open View proof of birth and Details.
  3. Tap ✓ Team reviewed if it's right, or Reject if something's wrong. The Conference President then certifies.
  4. Transfers: use the Transfer requests card — tap ✓ We accept this athlete. The former team and the Commissioner also sign off.

Need files? Export CSV, Hy-Tek roster and Download forms PDF are on the same tab.

📥Import athletes you registered somewhere elseTeam Manager roster, CSV or Excel
  1. Go to Team admin: roster & intake → Import athletes.
  2. Upload a Team Manager roster export (.zip or .tcl) or a CSV / Excel file from the other app.
  3. For CSV/Excel, check that each column is matched correctly, preview, then import. Athletes already on your roster are skipped.
  4. Each family gets a 6-letter code. Use Copy message to text or email it, or Download the codes (CSV) for all of them.
  5. Parents sign in, connect with the code (or automatically by email) and finish a short form. Finished athletes move to your normal review list.

Team Manager files have no parent contact info, so add a parent's email with Add parent email to link them automatically.

💵Set up team fees and paymentsTeam admins
  1. Open Team admin: roster & intake → Payments.
  2. Build your list with + Add a fee and + Add a discount: name each one (Registration, Uniform, Fundraiser, Parent volunteer fee, Sibling discount…), enter the amount, and choose who gets it — every athlete, once per family, each additional child, new or returning athletes only, or only athletes you pick. The box underneath shows what a family with 1, 2 or 3 athletes pays in total.
  3. Save. Parents see their total and every line. To change one athlete (take a fee off, add a scholarship), tap Fees on their row.
  4. For cards: Set up Stripe for this team (Stripe charges about 2.9% + 30¢ and deposits to your bank). Optionally add a payment plan.
  5. Add your Zelle, Cash App, Venmo and check/cash details. When parents tap I sent it, mark it Received ✓.
  6. Use Record payment, Adjust or Waive for anything handled in person.

Card payments, plans and tracking are part of the team's paid extras.

🏃Enter athletes into a meetLike Team Manager's "Entry by Event"
  1. On the Coach Dashboard open Meet registration & results and pick the meet.
  2. Choose a division, click an event and tick the athletes. Parent answers (✓ yes) and each athlete's best show beside the names.
  3. Relays: New Relay, then fill legs 1–4 (plus up to 4 alternates), or try Find Best Relay.
  4. Finish before the meet's Entries close time.

Limits per meet: Sub-Gremlin & Gremlin 3 events; Bantam, Juniors & Youth 4 (the 4th must include a relay); Intermediates 4. Only certified athletes can be entered, and entries stay locked until the league confirms your team's certification fees.

📋Set up meet volunteer jobsHost teams
  1. On the Coach Dashboard open Meet volunteers.
  2. List the jobs for your meet — as one list, per division, by time shift or custom rows.
  3. Visiting teams claim the open duties first come, first served. Anything still open by Thursday 8 PM is assigned automatically.
📺Add a livestream
  1. Start a YouTube Live and set it to Unlisted.
  2. In YouTube tap Share and copy the link.
  3. On the Coach Dashboard's Livestream card tap ＋ Add a livestream, paste the link and tap Add livestream. The replay stays after you end it.
💾Download a meet's Meet Manager backupFor your own files
  1. On the Coach Dashboard open Meet Manager backups, or tap Backup ⬇ next to a meet in Our schedule.
  2. Every team at that meet can download it.

Timers work from the Timer page. Results only ever come from Meet Manager — there's no typing results by hand.

🧰Before meet day
  1. Use Chrome or Edge on the timing laptop and sign in with your timer account.
  2. On the Timer page tap Download entries (Hy-Tek).
  3. In Meet Manager: File › Import › Semi-Colon Delimited Entries. Open Meet Manager in multi-user mode.
  4. Practice the connection with Test only so nothing posts publicly.
🔴Send results live during the meet
  1. On the Timer page choose Fully automatic: connect to the Meet Manager database and pick the meet's .mdb file (or use One key per event with F12).
  2. Look for ● Connected / ● Watching. The page checks every 30 seconds — leave it open.
  3. Re-sending an event replaces it; nothing is doubled.
  4. No live connection? Export a semicolon-delimited results file and use Upload the results file → Save these results.

Live results are a host-team paid extra. League-run meets are always live.

💾Upload the Meet Manager backup — every meetRequired, even if results went up live
  1. When the meet is over, send final results and tap Stop.
  2. In Meet Manager: File › Backup. This makes a file named like Tfmm6Bkup….zip.
  3. Upload that .zip in the meet's backup box. Results inside it are posted automatically.
  4. Fixed something later? Upload the newer backup — it replaces the whole meet's results.

Upload the Meet Manager backup, not a Team Manager results or entries export — those are refused. Meets with no backup show ⚠️ Backup needed.

🔀Mixed races (combined divisions)

Run mixed races in Meet Manager as usual. When results post, each runner is filed under their own division and gender and placed among them. Parents see "ran in the mixed race, Nth overall". Relays aren't re-filed.

↩️Posted something by mistake?

Ask your team admin to remove it. If a new upload would change results already posted, you'll be asked Yes, replace them with this file or No, keep what's posted. Only a site admin can remove a backup.

Still stuck? Check the FAQ, ask your team admin, or see league rules at valleyconference.org.
